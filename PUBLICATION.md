- Confirm the course permits publishing solutions and distributing the included starter code, tests, and exercise artifacts. The course website could not be verified during preparation; permission is not assumed.
- Preserve third-party notices, especially `weensyos/COPYRIGHT` and `weensyos/AUTHORS.md`. Do not apply a blanket license to code owned by others.
- Review the Git history before publication. It includes course staff commits, author email addresses, original assignment responses, and classmates' account identifiers. Editing current READMEs does not remove those from prior commits.
- Choose whether to retain the educational history or create a separately reviewed public snapshot. No history rewriting was performed.
- Review `timemachine/`, sample datasets, old implementation files, and saved output for relevance and redistribution permission. These files were retained rather than deleted without review.
- Review validation results and known limitations. No benchmark speedup, production readiness, or privacy compliance is claimed.
